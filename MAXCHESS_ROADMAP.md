# MaxChess Feature Roadmap

This roadmap records the features selected for future development on the MaxChess website.

## Playing and social
- Play against friends by invitation
- Rematch button
- Spectator chat

## Tournaments and community
- World MaxChess Championship
- School chess leagues
- Monthly championship
- Hall of Fame
- Tournament medals
- Player-of-the-month awards

## MaxChess experiences
- MaxChess TV
- Chess news desk
- Opening encyclopedia
- Interactive chess museum
- Achievements collection
- Community-created puzzles

## Larger MaxChess projects
- MaxChess Broadcast Network
- MaxChess Player Universe
- MaxChess Awards

## Scope notes
- These are planned features, not claims that the features are already implemented.
- Keep MaxChess free to play; do not add subscriptions, paywalls, or paid competitive advantages.
- Integrate future features into the existing MaxChess website rather than creating disconnected replacement sites.
- Preserve the existing MaxChess visual identity and avoid copying another chess site's branding or layout.

## Not selected
The following ideas were explicitly rejected in this planning discussion and are not included in this roadmap: Chess960, Three-check, King of the Hill, Bughouse, automatic game analysis, custom chess variants, and the proposed AI coach/trainer/opponent features.
