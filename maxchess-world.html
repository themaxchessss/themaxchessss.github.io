08 · OPENING ENCYCLOPEDIA

📚 Explore chess openings

Choose an opening to see its starting moves and the ideas behind it.

Italian Game · 1. e4 e5 2. Nf3 Nc6 3. Bc4

Develop quickly, control the center and prepare to castle. White often targets f7; Black should develop and contest the center.

Ruy López · 1. e4 e5 2. Nf3 Nc6 3. Bb5

White pressures the knight that supports e5. Both sides develop naturally and fight for central space.

Sicilian Defence · 1. e4 c5

Black challenges the center asymmetrically. Typical plans include queenside play and counterattacking the d4 square.

French Defence · 1. e4 e6 2. d4 d5

Black challenges White's center with ...d5. The light-squared bishop can be a strategic problem to solve.

Queen's Gambit · 1. d4 d5 2. c4

White offers the c-pawn to challenge Black's central pawn on d5. The gambit is usually declined or returned rather than held permanently.

London System · 1. d4 d5 2. Bf4

White develops the light-squared bishop early and builds a solid setup. Plans depend on Black's central structure.

09 · INTERACTIVE CHESS MUSEUM

🏛️ A short history of chess

Open an era to explore how the game developed.

Chaturanga · around the 6th century

An early Indian strategy game is widely regarded as an ancestor of chess. Its pieces represented different parts of an army.

Shatranj · early medieval period

Chess spread through Persia and the Islamic world. The names and movement of several pieces developed along the way.

Modern chess · 15th century

Major rule changes in Europe gave the queen and bishop their modern long-range movement, making the game faster and more tactical.

Organized competition · 19th century

Chess clubs, published analysis and international tournaments helped create modern competitive chess. The first recognized world championship match was held in 1886.

Computer chess · 20th century to today

Chess engines transformed preparation and analysis. Online play now lets people compete across the world.
