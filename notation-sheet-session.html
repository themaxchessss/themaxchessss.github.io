MaxChess notation key

Use this quick reference with every sheet. These are standard chess notation marks, arranged in the MaxChess reference style.

Piece letters

K King Q Queen R Rook B Bishop N Knight. Pawns have no letter.

Moves & outcomes

x capture + check # checkmate =Q promotion to queen.

Special moves

O-O kingside castle O-O-O queenside castle e.p. en passant.

Move annotations

! good !! brilliant ? mistake ?? blunder !? interesting ?! dubious.

Examples: Nf3 = knight to f3 · exd5 = e-pawn captures on d5 · Raxd1 = rook from the a-file captures on d1 · Qh7# = queen delivers checkmate.
