Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 14, 2026

This policy explains how MaxChess handles information when you use the MaxChess website at themaxchessss.github.io. MaxChess is intended to provide free online chess features. This page describes the practices visible in the site's published code; not every feature may be available to every visitor.

For young players: Ask a parent or guardian to read this policy with you. Do not put your home address, phone number, school, password, or other private information in a public profile, game chat, report, or other submission.

1. Information the site may handle

  • Account information: information needed to create and authenticate an account, including an email address held by the authentication provider and a username or other account metadata.
  • Profile and chess information: profile details, ratings, game positions and move histories, game results, tournament participation, and progress information where those features are used.
  • School features: if school/class features are used, class membership and game or roster information may be processed. Information shown to a teacher depends on the feature and sharing choices available in the site.
  • Connected Lichess information: if a player connects a Lichess account, MaxChess's server-side code is designed to store the linked Lichess username and an access token, and to record imported-game references. Treat a connected account as sharing access with MaxChess. Lichess has its own privacy policy and terms.
  • Technical information: the website host, authentication provider, browser, and network may process technical information such as IP address, request logs, and browser storage needed to deliver and secure the service. The exact logs and retention periods have not been independently confirmed here.

2. Why information is used

Information may be used to sign players in, operate chess games, save game state and progress, show profiles and ratings, support tournaments and school features, connect optional third-party services, protect the site, and diagnose problems.

3. Service providers and third parties

The website is published through GitHub Pages. The site's code uses Supabase for database and authentication functions. Some optional account-closure email code can use Resend if the site owner has configured it. These providers process information under their own terms and privacy practices. MaxChess does not control their independent services.

If you choose to connect Lichess, information is also handled by Lichess under its own policies. Do not connect an account unless you and your parent or guardian understand the permissions requested.

4. Public and shared information

Information displayed in public profiles, leaderboards, tournaments, or other public-facing features may be visible to other visitors. School features may show selected player information to the teacher who owns a class. Avoid entering information you would not want other people to see.

5. Account closure and deletion

The currently published account-closure function does not perform a complete permanent deletion of every record. It attempts to remove certain profile, matchmaking, friendship, and tournament-participant records, ends active games, and disables sign-in by placing a long-duration ban on the authentication account. Game records and some other records may remain. A connected Lichess token may also remain because the authentication account is disabled rather than fully deleted.

For that reason, do not interpret the site's account-closure feature as a guarantee that all personal information is erased. A parent or guardian should contact the site owner to ask about a specific data request. Do not post private account details in a public issue or report.

6. Security and retention

MaxChess uses access controls in its database and server-side functions for some sensitive operations. No website or internet transmission can be guaranteed completely secure. Information may be kept for as long as needed to operate the service, maintain records, resolve disputes, or meet legal and security needs. Exact retention periods have not been published.

7. Children's privacy

MaxChess may be used by young chess players. A parent or guardian should supervise account creation and use, review connected-service permissions, and help with privacy or deletion requests. The site should not be used to submit sensitive personal information. This policy is not a statement that MaxChess has completed any particular child-privacy legal compliance review.

8. Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated as MaxChess changes. The date at the top indicates the latest revision. Please review it periodically with a parent or guardian.

9. Questions

For privacy questions or requests, ask a parent or guardian to contact the MaxChess site owner through a contact method they have independently verified. Do not send passwords, access tokens, or other secrets.
