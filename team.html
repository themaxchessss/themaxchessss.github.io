Build MaxChess together

♛ Join the MaxChess Team

Help make free chess more fun, welcoming, useful, and accessible. Choose a role that matches what you enjoy doing.

These are volunteer/community contributor roles, not paid jobs. There is no promise of payment or employment. If you are under 18, get a parent or guardian's permission before applying. Never include your address, phone number, school, passwords, or private account details.
