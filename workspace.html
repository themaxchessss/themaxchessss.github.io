What makes a good puzzle?

  • The position is legal and reachable.
  • There is a clear best move or sequence.
  • The intended theme is genuinely present.
  • The solution includes best defense, not just a hopeful line.
  • The explanation teaches the idea without giving away the answer first.

Zwischenzug checklist

A zwischenzug is an in-between move: instead of making the expected recapture or reply, the player inserts a stronger move first.

  1. Show the apparent recapture or expected move.
  2. Find the forcing check, attack, or threat in between.
  3. Explain why the opponent must respond.
  4. Show what the player wins or improves afterward.

Review workflow

Draft → self-check → reviewer check → editor approval → publish. Never mark a draft as published just because it has been saved here. If you work with a team, send the exported JSON to the MaxChess project owner/editor using the team's approved channel.
